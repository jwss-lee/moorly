English

Moorly does not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. We do not operate any server for the app, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

  • Your connections: Moorly connects directly from your Mac to the servers and cloud services you add (SFTP, FTP, WebDAV, S3, OneDrive, Dropbox). Files, file lists and logs travel only between your Mac and those services.
  • Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. When you sign in to Microsoft or Dropbox, the sign-in happens on the provider's own page; Moorly only receives an access token, which stays in your Keychain.
  • Local data: connection settings, the optional file-search index (file names, sizes, dates and checksums), recent folders, favorites and log-watch settings are stored only on your Mac, inside the app's container. Turning off file search deletes the index. Deleting a connection deletes its stored data.
  • Finder and local folders: to list recent folders, Moorly asks Finder which folder its front window shows (only while Finder is in front, and only if you allow Automation). Folders you add as local folders (such as iCloud Drive or a Google Drive app folder) are read on your Mac with the access you grant. These paths and file names stay on your Mac.
  • Notifications for log watches are created on your Mac by macOS.
  • Your use of each server or cloud service is governed by that provider's own privacy policy.

Questions: giveing7777@gmail.com

  • Mitteilungen der Log-Überwachung erzeugt macOS auf Ihrem Mac.
  • Für die Nutzung der einzelnen Server und Cloud-Dienste gelten die Datenschutzrichtlinien der jeweiligen Anbieter.
